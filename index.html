Frequently Asked Questions
When should I book my alterations?+

We recommend booking your first appointment approximately 2–3 months before your wedding date. Some gowns may require more time, particularly if extensive alterations are needed. If you are unsure when to book, please contact us to discuss your wedding date and gown.

How many fittings will I need?+

Most brides will require between two and three fittings, depending on the gown and the alterations required. The number of appointments will be discussed during your initial consultation.

Should I bring my wedding shoes?+

Yes. Please bring the shoes you intend to wear on your wedding day, or shoes with a similar heel height — this can affect the length and overall fit of your gown.

Should I bring my undergarments or shapewear?+

Yes. We recommend bringing the undergarments and shapewear you plan to wear with your gown, so it can be fitted as accurately as possible.

Do you accept last-minute alterations?+

Last-minute appointments may be available depending on our schedule and the alterations required. Please contact us as soon as possible.

Do I need an appointment?+

Yes. All fittings and consultations are by appointment only, allowing dedicated time and attention for each bride.

How does booking actually work?+

Choosing a time below sends a request — it isn't confirmed automatically. Jane reviews each request and confirms by email once your slot is secured; you'll also see it as a calendar invite once accepted.
